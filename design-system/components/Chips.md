# Chips

Chips, filter chips and reaction-family badges.

- `.chip` — mono 11.5px info tag on a 6% `--ink` wash (class names, levels, mechanisms).
- `.fchip` — a toggle pill with `aria-pressed`; pressed = `--ink` fill. Family filters add `t-redox`, `t-subst`, `t-addn`, `t-elim`, `t-cond`, `t-acid` to fill with that family's colour.
- `.badge` — mono uppercase outline in the family colour: `f-redox`, `f-subst`, `f-addn`, `f-elim`, `f-cond`, `f-acid` (organic) or `redox`, `ligand`, `acidbase`, `amph` (inorganic). `.badge.solid` fills it.
