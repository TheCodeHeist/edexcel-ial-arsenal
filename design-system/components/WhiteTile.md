# WhiteTile

The white tile: the always-light lab surface behind test tubes, apparatus and colour chemistry.

- `.white-tile` = `--tilebg` with a 44px `--tilegrid` grid, `--r-lg`, inset shadow. **It stays light in every theme**, so chemical colours read true; ink on it is `--tile-ink` (#33424a), never `--ink`.
- Tubes are SVG: 1.6px outlines, a liquid path whose `fill` transitions (1.3s) from the reagent colour to the result, precipitates as dot clusters, a silver mirror, rising bubbles, fumes, litmus strips.
- Caption each tube with the reagent in mono and the observation in Geist; positives in bold.
